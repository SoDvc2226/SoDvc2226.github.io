Research

My research focuses on:

  • Structure exploitation in polynomial and semidefinite optimization, enabling applications to machine learning problems such as neural network certification and mixture model identification.
  • Employing Christoffel–Darboux kernel as a means to strengthen relaxation bounds and/or obtain exact solutions at low hierarchy orders, thus making (even dense) semidefinite relaxations more computationally tractable.

Publications

  • Mixtures Closest to a Given Measure: A Semidefinite Programming Approach, Preprint, 2025.
  • Leveraging Christoffel-Darboux Kernels to Strengthen Moment-SOS Relaxations, Preprint, 2025.
  • Verifying Properties of Binary Neural Networks Using Sparse Polynomial Optimization, ICLR, 2025.
